Sum up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image.

A one-page newspaper front page — **THE WORLD** — rendered to a 1024×1024
PNG. Lead story: Russia's overnight attack on Ukraine and the missiles that
fell on Poland, with NATO jets scrambled. Also inside: the world court ruling
Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide, the S&P 500 entering correction, a 6.8
earthquake in central Peru, the COP31 climate summit deadlocked on fossil
fuels, KL Rahul's SENA centuries, and the UK–EU post-Brexit deal.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image was drawn at build time by rendering an HTML
page to PNG — no AI image generator, no network at run time.
